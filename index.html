Install

Three steps.

Download

Get Foxy.exe. No installer.

Run it

Foxy isn't signed, so Windows may warn you. Click More info → Run anyway.

Say hi

Foxy shows up at the top of your screen. Right click the tray icon for settings.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes.

What do friends see?

Your name, your fox, whether you're online and the song you're playing.

How does it update?

On its own. You can turn that off in Settings.

How do I remove it?

Quit from the tray icon and delete the exe.
