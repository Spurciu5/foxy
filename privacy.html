Privacy Policy

Foxy Companion · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Foxy is a small desktop companion made by one person. This page explains what data Foxy uses and why.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, clipboard history, shelf files, reminders, notifications shown in the notch, and your stats (like listening time and the number of key presses and clicks) are stored only on your computer. Foxy counts how many keys you press, but never which keys. None of this is sent anywhere.

What is sent to the Foxy server

If you use the friends features, Foxy registers you on its server and stores:

  • the name you choose and your friend code
  • your friends list and friend requests
  • game invites and game state while a game is in progress

While you are online, your friends can see your name, your fox's look, whether you are online, and the song you are currently playing. Pokes, visits and game moves are passed between you and your friends through the server. The server runs on Cloudflare.

Music and lyrics

To show lyrics, Foxy sends the title and artist of the song you are playing to LRCLIB (lrclib.net). To open a song in Spotify, it may look up the song on Apple's iTunes Search and song.link. No account or personal information is sent with these requests.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the Foxy website, your message and (only if you give it) your email address are stored so the developer can read them and reply. Your IP address is not stored; only a one-way hash of it is kept to prevent spam.

What Foxy does not do

Foxy has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time through the feedback form. Uninstalling Foxy removes the data stored on your computer.

Children

Foxy is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
